Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

Summary

Citizenship Prep is designed to work without an account. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking, and it does not send your study data or notes to the developer or any third party.

Data stored on your device

Your study progress, bookmarks, practice history, settings, optional first name for the greeting, and interview preparation notes are stored only on your device. They may be included in your device backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings. You can delete progress and notes at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Pro Lifetime is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple processes your payment; the developer does not receive your payment details. The app receives a signed transaction record from Apple to confirm your purchase on your device.

Speech

Listening features use the voice built into iOS on your device. The app does not record audio or use the microphone.

Links

Links to USCIS and other websites open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If these practices change, this policy will be updated in the app and on the published policy page before the change takes effect.
