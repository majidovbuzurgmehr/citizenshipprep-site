How do I restore my Pro Lifetime purchase?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro Lifetime. Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase; there is no subscription to manage or cancel.

Which civics test version should I study?

USCIS gives the 2008 civics test to applicants who filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025, and the 2025 test to applicants who filed on or after that date. You can change the version in Settings at any time.

Are the questions official?

Civics questions and answers are reproduced from official USCIS documents, with a link to the source on every question. Answers that change with elections or appointments are marked and show the date they were last verified. Always check uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview.

Where is my data stored?

Your progress and interview notes are stored only on your device. The app has no account, ads, or tracking. You can delete your progress and notes in Settings.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
